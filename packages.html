Common Questions

We edit short-form video content including Instagram Reels, TikToks, and YouTube Shorts. This includes cuts, transitions, text overlays, captions, and on-brand formatting. For Growth and Pro packages, we also include caption design and title overlays. Full long-form video production is available as a separate add-on.

All paid advertising campaigns are run directly through your own social media profiles and ad accounts. You retain full ownership and visibility of your campaigns at all times. We provide the strategy, audience targeting, and ad copy — the spend goes through your account so everything stays transparent.

Payment is due at the end of each month on an agreed date. We establish a clear payment schedule during onboarding so you always know when your invoice is due and can plan accordingly.

All packages require a minimum 3-month commitment to ensure we can build strategy, create content, and deliver measurable results. Month-to-month arrangements are available after the initial term at a +15% premium.

All content — including edited videos, graphics, and captions — is shared with you for review and approval 5 days before it is scheduled to go live. You can request revisions within your package allowance before publishing.
